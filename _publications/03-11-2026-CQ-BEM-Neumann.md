In this paper, we consider three-dimensional exterior wave propagation problems with non-homogeneous
Neumann boundary conditions. We reformulate the problem as a space-time boundary integral equation
involving a hypersingular operator. To solve the resulting formulation, we combine Lubich's convolution
quadrature with a shifted collocation boundary element discretization and investigate numerically the
efficiency, stability, and convergence of the resulting numerical scheme. Numerical tests show that the
proposed approach achieves an accuracy comparable to that of Lubich's convolution quadrature combined with
a Galerkin boundary element method, and provide numerical evidence of the unconditional stability of the
collocation scheme.

**Keywords:** 3D wave equation, Neumann problem, space-time boundary integral
equation, convolution quadrature, collocation methods
